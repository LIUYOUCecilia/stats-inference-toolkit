## FAQ

### What data can you analyze?

Conversion rates, revenue, session duration, survey responses, categorical feedback, and other experiment metrics.

### What if my experiment has more than two groups?

This demo supports 2-group A/B tests, 3+ group numeric ANOVA with Tukey post-hoc comparisons, and multi-group Chi-Square for categorical outcomes.

### Do you only provide code?

No. The main deliverable is a readable PDF report with statistical interpretation and a plain-English business recommendation.
